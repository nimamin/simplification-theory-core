# Two moving Escentrons: variable-field reference pilot (v0.27)

Two independent moving particles are integrated with the current **projection-cancellation** energy allocator. Neither is frozen. Fields are recalculated at every ODE evaluation using the current positions, masses and pulses. No gravity-to-levitation energy transfer or artificial damping is introduced. This is a Python scientific pilot, **not** the verified Rust kernel.

## Initial setup

- `G=50, E_c=10, k_g=k_l=k_k=1` for each particle.
- Particle A: `m=0.1, p=9.9, v=0` (low mass).
- Particle B: `m=9, p=1, v=0` (low pulse).
- Both start on a line, symmetrically placed around the origin.
- Instantaneous equilibrium distance outside the inner-core regularization: `r_eq=k_l*p_A*p_B/(k_g*m_A*m_B)=11`.
- For each particle, `a_g=(E_g/E_c)f_g`, `a_l=(E_l/E_c)f_l`; the energy transfer follows `allocation.rs` using `a_i dot (a_g+a_l)` with negative contributions canceled. Continuous kinetic derivative is `2*k_k*v dot (a_g+a_l)`.
- Integrated with SciPy DOP853 at `rtol=2e-10`, `atol=1e-12`, `max_step=0.25`, with a bounded experiment window, not a guarantee of perpetual convergence.

## Findings

| Initial separation | Duration | Distance range | Turning points | Conclusion |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 11.0 | 200 | 11.0 | 0 | Exactly stationary; both reservoirs unchanged |
| 11.55 | 200 | 10.5718–11.55 | 5 | Attraction then oscillation |
| 10.45 | 200 | 10.45–11.5765 | 5 | Repulsion then oscillation |
| 5.5 | 200 | 5.5–17.7815 | 7 | Strong repulsion but no permanent escape in window |
| 1.1 | 40 | 1.1–1.5447 | 136 | Rapid small-radius oscillation, **not** escape |
| 22.0 | 200 | 10.0362–22.0 | 3 | Attraction, return, oscillation |

At exact balance, both particles remain stationary with no energy changes. In perturbed cases energies are positive at accepted recorded states, and total per-particle energy residuals are around `1e-14` for most cases; at the especially stiff 1.1 separation, drift increases to `2.5e-9`. No empirical evidence of asymptotic settling or escape was found for these initial conditions and finite times. The initial equality distance is **not necessarily** the instantaneous equilibrium distance as both reservoirs evolve.

A tighter independent numerical comparison changes the full endpoint state by about `1.9e-8` for the 11.55 case, `2.9e-9` for 10.45, and `1.1e-5` for 5.5; stronger convergence tests are necessary before treating close approaches as high-precision reference data.

The output `variable_field_windows.csv` contains 1,844 variable-field input/output windows for the **next** neural phase. All windows are from six trajectories and are **highly correlated**; they must NOT be randomly split into training and test. Hold out full initial-configuration trajectories (or groups of parameter values) and generate much broader initial conditions. Every window is generated by interpolation of the integrated trajectory, so it is *exploratory* supervision, not exact ground truth. The neutral case contributes stationary controls.

The scripts, trajectories, checks, figures, summary CSV, and candidate training-window CSV are provided as the conversation artifact `escentron_two_body_experiment.zip`. Their presence here is documentation of an independent pilot, **not** evidence that this experiment was added to or run by GitHub CI.
